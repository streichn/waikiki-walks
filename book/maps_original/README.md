# Original map files from the cartographer

Walk 11.pdf and Walk 12.pdf are the cartographer's original map files for "Waikīkī: Twelve Walks Through Time".
Jason Nakano (Fruits Media) made the maps in 2022 and shared them with Veneeta Acson on 19 Nov 2026 in a Google
Drive folder named "Waikiki walks"; she forwarded his email to Nick the same day.

The book prints Walks XI and XII as two-page foldouts, and our book PDF has only the first half of each. These two
files show each map whole, as vector drawings, so scripts/georef/extra_maps.py reads every printed stop number and
street name directly. They supply the pins for Walk XI stops 7 to 45 and Walk XII stops 15 to 28. Stops the printed
book pages already show keep their pins from the book pages.

The same Drive folder also has originals for Walks 1 to 10 (Walk 10 in two parts), a master map, and two images
(Helumoa, O'ahu Kona ahupua'a). They are not copied here because the book PDF already covers those walks.
Use of these files is covered by the same publisher permission as the book (legal/CHECKLIST.md).
