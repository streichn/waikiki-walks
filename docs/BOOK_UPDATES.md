- II-12: Lappert's Hawaiʻi is still at the Hilton Hawaiian Village.
- III-2: Kani Ka Pila Grille at the Outrigger Reef is open, with live Hawaiian music nightly.
- IV-17: Macy's Waikīkī is open.
- X-39: Waiʻoli Kitchen and Bake Shop is open (Tuesday to Sunday).
- All walks: TheBus, 808-848-5555, press 2 for route information, is unchanged.
