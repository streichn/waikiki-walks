# Book updates since the printed edition

Two lists, kept for the next printing and for the app.
Part A records changes Veneeta has made to the text of *Waikīkī: Twelve Walks Through Time*.
Part B lists statements that may have gone out of date, with what a check found; each needs her decision before the text changes.
App-only fixes (pin positions, map layout, parsing errors) are in docs/FEEDBACK.md, not here.

### Checked and still accurate

- I-5: the Ala Wai pedestrian bridge to Kālaimoku Street is still planned (city design-build project "Ala Pono"; federal agreement signed 2025).
- II-12: Lappert's Hawaiʻi is still at the Hilton Hawaiian Village.
- III-2: Kani Ka Pila Grille at the Outrigger Reef is open, with live Hawaiian music nightly.
- IV-17: Macy's Waikīkī is open.
- X-39: Waiʻoli Kitchen and Bake Shop is open (Tuesday to Sunday).
- All walks: TheBus, 808-848-5555, press 2 for route information, is unchanged.
