# Draft request to Island Heritage

Subject: Permission request: app edition of "Waikīkī: Twelve Walks Through Time"

Dear Island Heritage,

I am the author of "Waikīkī: Twelve Walks Through Time" (First Edition, 2023; ISBN 978-1-61710-544-9). I would like to publish a companion smartphone app that guides walkers along the twelve walks, using the book's text, maps, and photographs, with GPS directions and read-aloud narration. The app would credit Island Heritage and the book, link to where the book can be purchased, and be sold through the Apple App Store and Google Play by subscription.

Could you let me know whether Island Heritage holds the digital and electronic rights to the book's text and images, and if so, what terms you would require for a license? I would also welcome the chance to promote the print book within the app.

Mahalo,
Veneeta Acson, Ph.D.
