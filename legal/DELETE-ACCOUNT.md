## Waikīkī Walks: delete your account and data

Waikīkī Walks is published by [OWNER NAME] on Google Play and the App Store.

**In the app (fastest):** open Settings and tap **Delete my account**. Your account is deleted right away.

**Without the app:** email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

**What we delete:** your account (name and email), walk progress, saved stops, and app settings. Nothing is kept.

**What stays with Apple or Google:** your purchases belong to your Apple ID or Google account, not to us. Deleting your Waikīkī Walks account does not refund or cancel them, and you can get them back later with Restore purchases. Waikīkī Walks has no auto-renewing subscriptions, so there is nothing to cancel.

**Signed in with Apple?** Deleting in the app also disconnects Sign in with Apple. If you ask by email instead, you can disconnect it yourself: on your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Sign in with Apple, choose Waikīkī Walks, and tap Delete.
